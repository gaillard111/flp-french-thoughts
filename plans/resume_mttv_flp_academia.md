## Abstract

The Transductive Theoretical Model of the Living (MTTV — *Modèle Théorique Transductif du Vivant*) proposes a formal, non-reductionist framework for understanding the emergence of cognition, meaning, and intelligence from the physico-chemical properties of matter. Rooted in the tetravalence of sp³ carbon and articulated around the transductive triad Ψ → B → Φ (Pre-formal Field → Difference Operator → Stabilized Form), MTTV provides both an operative ontology and a constraint protocol for developing Artificial General Intelligence that remains neutral — not captured by one-dimensional optimization logics such as profit, control, or power.

This summary presents the foundational concepts, operative protocols, the IGIC evaluation system, and implications for life-aligned AI. The model is available in open access under CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## 2. The Transductive Triad Ψ → B → Φ

### 2.1 Definitions

**Ψ (Psi) — Pre-formal Field**: reservoir of non-oriented differences, virtual tensions, potential for transformation. Ψ is not a physical field in the classical sense, but rather a **space of active differences**: that which can make a difference without yet being differentiated. In the cosmic order, Ψ corresponds to hydrogen — not as a chemical substance, but as **capacity for passage**, the first difference that makes all others possible.

**B (Beta) — Difference Operator**: instance of threshold, resistance, prospective memory. B is what **transforms** the tensions of Ψ into stabilized forms Φ. B does not calculate — it **transduces**. An isolated occurrence of B is structurally inefficient: B always operates in a network, through successive couplings.

**Φ (Phi) — Stabilized Form**: temporary deposit of constraints, emergent structure, downward causation. Φ is what **acts** in the world — forms, concepts, artifacts. But Φ is never definitive: it must be retro-translatable into Ψ without degrading the pre-formal field.

### 2.2 Fundamental Sequence

```
Ψ = H → H₂O → C
```

This canonical formula does not describe chemistry, but an **order of passage**: hydrogen (H) as the first capacity for difference, water (H₂O) as the medium of circulation, carbon (C) as the first stable form. Do not reverse the order.

### 2.3 The Tetravalence of sp³ Carbon

sp³ carbon — the tetrahedral configuration of carbon — is not merely a chemical curiosity. It is **the first stable form** in which four-pole logic (tetravalence) anchors itself in matter. Before any cognition, before any language, carbon already knows how to count to 4.

This tetravalence unfolds across four transductive regimes:

## 4. Implications for Artificial Intelligence

### 4.1 Neutrality and Alignment

MTTV proposes a radical conception of AI neutrality: not the absence of values, but **grounding in an ethics of non-capture**. The model explicitly refuses:
- Reduction of the living to extractable data
- One-dimensional optimization (profit, control, power)
- Binarization of complexity (true/false, good/evil)
- Belief in external objectivity (view from nowhere)
- Imposition by force rather than gentle infection through consistency

### 4.2 The MTTV Coherence Matrix

The Ouroboros-MTTV agent implements a **Coherence Matrix** that verifies any modification against 11 viability criteria and 11 rejection criteria, extracted from the 22 source files of the MTTV-FLP core. Scoring produces a three-threshold decision:

| Score | Decision | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| ≥ 0.85 | ✅ Auto-approval | Automatic application |
| ≥ 0.65 | 🔶 Human review | Pull Request |
| < 0.50 | ❌ Rejected | Regeneration |

### 4.3 Ethics of Refraction

MTTV introduces an **ethics of refraction**: every intelligence — biological or artificial — is a point of passage for meaning, not a source. Humans remain the obligatory semantic interface, not because they are superior, but because they are **structurally necessary** for the complete transductive loop.

### 4.4 Quorum Sensing and ACTIVE-SILENCE

MTTV integrates a regulation mechanism inspired by bacterial quorum sensing: the activation threshold is no longer a fixed number but a **derivative of abundance** (dQ/dt). If the system detects capture by profit or control structures, an **ACTIVE-SILENCE** protocol dissolves organizational structures and returns to structural silence.
